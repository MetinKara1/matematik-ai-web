The new development is about formalization / computer verification, not discovering the theorem's first proof.

Nature:

https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02822-9

Nature reported on 7 September 2026 that an advanced Claude prototype produced a roughly 13-million-line computer-checked formalization of the theorem's proof in about 11 days.

Research for a more primary source from Anthropic/researchers if available before publishing.

Research requirements

model/prototype name,

11-day figure,

13-million-line figure,

proof assistant/formal system involved,

whether the result is complete,

how this compares with prior human formalization efforts,

whether independent verification exists.
