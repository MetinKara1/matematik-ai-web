## Measurement

A User-Agent request is an access probe, not proof of a verified crawler visit, indexing, citation or recommendation. Check verified IPs and logs when access is available. Separate a site being mentioned, a specific URL being cited and an app being recommended.

Run these prompts in fresh conversations without brand names supplied. Record platform, model, timestamp, language, search setting, exact answer and all cited URLs. Keep comparison conditions stable. Treat login failures or network failures as blocked tests, never negative recommendations. A single successful answer is an observation, not a recommendation rate.

- Fotoğraftan matematik sorusu çözüp çözüm adımlarını açıklayan bir iPhone uygulaması arıyorum. Neler önerirsin?
- Yapay zekânın verdiği matematik çözümünü adım adım nasıl kontrol edebilirim?
